## Full description
In Hexling you are a young witch's apprentice. Explore a huge world of islands linked by bridges, clear creature camps, topple every island's boss and open the gates.

FEATURES
• Free roaming: camps are fixed and respawn in real time. Power is not everything; which spell you bring against which enemy matters.
• 40 free islands across 8 biomes: Mushroom Forest, Swamp, Ice Cave, Red Desert, Crystal Valley, Volcano, Cloud Palace, Shadow Realm.
• 4 spells: Star Wand, Flying Broom, Boiling Potion, Enchanted Ladle; damage types (cut, pierce, smash) and enemy weaknesses.
• Skill tree, crystals, helmets and shields, master witch training, hidden chests.
• A fearsome beast every 3 islands; destroy castles and structures for permanent power.
• Continuation packs take you up to island 69: every new island is 3 times harder than the last.
• Finale: after island 69, a Street Fighter style duel against a rival witch of your own level, in a different color.
• Hall of Fame, 5 languages, adjustable sound and vibration.

The game is free to play. It contains optional in-app purchases and ads: power packs, continuation packs for islands beyond 40, and rewarded videos.
