## Full description
In Hexling you are a young witch's apprentice. Explore a huge world of islands linked by bridges, clear creature camps, topple every island's boss and unravel the mystery of the broken mirror.

ADVENTURE
• Free roaming: camps are fixed and respawn in real time. Power is not everything; which spell you bring against which enemy matters.
• 40 free islands across 8 biomes: Mushroom Forest, Swamp, Ice Cave, Red Desert, Crystal Valley, Volcano, Cloud Palace, Shadow Realm.
• 4 spells: Star Wand, Flying Broom, Boiling Potion, Enchanted Ladle. Master damage types (cut, pierce, smash), enemy weaknesses and spell combos (+30% damage).
• Skill tree, crystals, helmets and shields, master witch training, hidden chests.
• A fearsome beast every 3 islands; destroy castles and structures for permanent power.
• Journal: every boss unlocks a page of the broken mirror. A 69-page mystery reveals who the rival witch really is.

WITCH HOUSE
• Pet the cat, water the garden, brew potions: chores at home grant permanent power.
• The cauldron collects souls while you are away; come back daily and your login streak grows the rewards.

FIGHTING ARENA
• Street Fighter style side-view duels: punch, kick, spell, block, jump.
• Beat 12 legendary witches in order. Send your ghost code to a friend and let them fight your shadow.
• A new weekly challenge with special rules: no blocking, magic only, glass cannon, fast week.
• Unlock witch outfits as you win.

FINALE
• Continuation packs take you up to island 69: every new island is 3 times harder than the last. At the end, a duel against a rival witch of your own level, in a different color.

Hall of Fame, 5 languages, adjustable sound and vibration.

The game is free to play. It contains optional in-app purchases and ads: power packs, continuation packs for islands beyond 40, and rewarded videos.
